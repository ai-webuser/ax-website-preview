A managed branch network, built around your needs.

SD-WAN uses software-defined policies to manage traffic across available WAN connections. Axclusive plans to combine a Fortinet-based solution with access connectivity and an agreed managed service scope.

This page is for requirements discussions. Launch timing, packages, devices, subscriptions, support and service commitments are not yet confirmed. Broadband is intended as part of the managed offering, not a standalone shared business broadband product.

Resilience is a design choice

Two links do not automatically mean two independent paths.

SD-WAN can select paths using configured policies and supported health measurements. It does not create extra physical capacity, remove congestion from every destination or guarantee uninterrupted application sessions.

Confirm shared risks, failover behaviour and application recovery during testing. A single session should not be assumed to combine the full speed of both links.

Security is part of the scope

Fortinet Secure SD-WAN integrates networking and security capabilities. The selected device, subscriptions and configuration determine what is enabled.

No Fortinet partner designation, fixed security package or availability guarantee is claimed for this planned service.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Can I order this SD-WAN service now?+
The offering is planned. You can discuss requirements, but launch availability, packages and delivery commitments are not yet confirmed.
Will it be bundled with Axclusive DIA or broadband?+
That is the intended model. Broadband would be provided within the managed solution; we are not presenting a standalone shared business broadband product.
Does SD-WAN replace a leased line?+
Not in every design. SD-WAN manages traffic across underlying links. A private leased line and Internet access have different connectivity characteristics; choose based on the application and security requirements.
Does dual connectivity guarantee no downtime?+
No. Links can share infrastructure, and failover can affect sessions. Physical diversity, equipment, power and application recovery need to be designed and tested.
What are the prices and support hours?+
They are not yet confirmed. The launch proposal will identify hardware, subscriptions, access circuits, implementation and ongoing support.
