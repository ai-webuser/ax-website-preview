Questions about Axclusive

Find the service that matches the job your network needs to do.

What does Axclusive provide in Singapore?+
Axclusive is an enterprise Internet service provider and network infrastructure company. Services include Dedicated Internet Access, IP transit, leased lines, data centre connectivity and managed services, with scope agreed for each project.
Can you connect an address outside the data centre directory?+
Yes. The directory is a reference list, not the limit of our Singapore-wide connectivity. Send us your business address or both endpoints so we can confirm availability, building access and delivery requirements.
Do I need DIA, a leased line or IP transit?+
DIA connects your business to public Internet destinations. A leased line connects agreed private endpoints. IP transit is relevant to networks with their own routing requirements. Your application, addressing and network design determine the suitable service.
Can Axclusive help with Cisco equipment as well as connectivity?+
Yes. Discuss Catalyst switching, Nexus, routers, firewalls and collaboration alongside your connectivity. The Cisco solutions page explains the capabilities and how to scope a project.
How do I get a useful quotation?+
Share your location or endpoints, required service, bandwidth or applications, and target date. For equipment work, include current models, licences and the problem you want to solve. The team can then establish the scope and commercial options.
