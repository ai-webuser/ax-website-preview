Questions before your Cisco project

Cisco solutions FAQ

Define the outcome, the supported environment and the scope before committing.

Which Cisco areas can Axclusive help with?+
Our capabilities cover Catalyst switches, Cisco routers and routing, Nexus switches, firewalls/security and collaboration. Specific models, software, licences and configurations are confirmed as part of the project scope.
Can you review an existing Cisco network?+
Share the current models, configuration context, software versions and the issue you want to address. We can discuss a review and determine what is supported, what can be retained and where changes may be required.
Do I need to replace every switch during an upgrade?+
Not necessarily. Port capacity, uplinks, features, support lifecycle and the actual bottleneck should guide the decision. A targeted upgrade or configuration change may be appropriate.
Are Cisco licences and vendor support included?+
Confirm the exact licences, subscription terms and vendor-support entitlement in your quotation. Axclusive implementation or managed support and Cisco vendor support are separate scopes; do not assume one includes the other.
Can equipment and connectivity be planned together?+
Yes. Discuss Cisco equipment alongside DIA, leased lines, data centre connectivity or IP transit as appropriate. The proposal should identify the equipment, circuit and support responsibilities.
Is ongoing management part of every deployment?+
Ongoing monitoring and management are agreed separately. Confirm coverage hours, supported equipment, change management and escalation arrangements before ordering.
