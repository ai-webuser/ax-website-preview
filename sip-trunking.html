What is SIP trunking?

The connection behind your business number.

A SIP trunk connects a compatible business phone system to the public telephone network over IP. Your PBX handles extensions and call flows; the SIP trunk supplies the external calling connection.

Three quantities matter: users who need an extension, numbers people can dial, and channels for simultaneous external calls. They are related, but they are not interchangeable.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Is Axclusive the SIP trunk provider?+
Yes. Axclusive is licensed by IMDA to provide IP telephony services and supplies the SIP trunk. We can also manage a cloud PBX when that service is included in your proposal.
Can I keep my existing business numbers?+
Number porting is subject to eligibility, correct ownership details and coordination with the existing provider. Porting is charged separately. Keep the current service active until the migration plan is confirmed.
Do I need one SIP channel per employee?+
Not necessarily. Channel sizing depends on simultaneous external calls, including relevant transfer and forwarding scenarios. Employee count alone does not determine peak call demand.
Is a PBX included with SIP trunking?+
A trunk-only service supplies voice connectivity to a compatible system. If you need the phone system operated for you, request a separate managed cloud PBX scope powered by Yeastar.
Can I use my existing Internet connection?+
We can review bandwidth, latency, jitter, packet loss and the LAN or firewall setup. Call quality depends on the complete path, including endpoints and local connectivity.
Where can I estimate monthly SIP spending?+
Use the existing Axclusive SIP trunk pricing calculator for channels, DDI blocks and estimated usage. Confirm the final service scope and all additional charges in the quotation.
