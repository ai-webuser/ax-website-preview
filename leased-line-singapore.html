The connection between your locations

What is a leased line?

A leased line is a private connection between specified business locations, with dedicated bandwidth and agreed service commitments. Axclusive’s leased line services connect sites without sending that inter-site traffic over public Internet routes.

Think of it as the link between your office and the systems your team relies on. It provides site-to-site connectivity; Internet access is a separate requirement.

Leased Line FAQ

Make your next connectivity decision with the right questions answered.

What is the difference between a leased line and an Internet leased line?+
The term “Internet leased line” can refer to dedicated Internet access. This page describes Axclusive’s private site-to-site leased line service. Tell us whether you need to connect specific locations or reach public Internet destinations; the right design may include both.
What bandwidth options does Axclusive offer?+
Axclusive advertises symmetrical leased line bandwidth from 1Gbps to 100Gbps. Availability and final configuration depend on the endpoints and agreed service design.
How do I compare leased line providers for low latency?+
Compare commitments for the actual endpoints and route, including how latency is measured and whether resilience changes the path. Share the response times your applications require and request those details in the proposal.
Is encryption included?+
Encryption is optional. A private path does not itself encrypt your data. Discuss Axclusive’s end-to-end AES-256 encryption option and confirm its scope, equipment and management requirements.
Can the connection support redundancy?+
Discuss diverse paths, access circuits and failover as part of the design. Confirm which physical routes, equipment or building entries are shared, and agree testing for the proposed backup arrangement.
Can I connect more than two offices or overseas locations?+
Share all endpoints so the team can review a suitable arrangement of private connections. Axclusive has network presence beyond Singapore, but service availability for a specific address or international route needs to be confirmed.
How much does a Singapore leased line cost?+
Request a quote for your exact endpoints, bandwidth and selected options. Compare recurring charges and installation costs alongside the SLA, equipment responsibilities and contract terms. If you mean Internet access rather than private connectivity, ask for a DIA quotation.
How long does installation take?+
Lead time depends on circuit availability, building access and any new delivery work. Request an endpoint-specific timeline and coordinate activation with your existing contract and migration plan.
