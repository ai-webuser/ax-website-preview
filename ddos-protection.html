What is Magic Transit?

Protect the network before the flood reaches it.

Cloudflare Magic Transit is a network security service that protects routed IP infrastructure against DDoS attacks. Axclusive helps you assess how it fits your connectivity and how protected traffic reaches your services.

Start with the public systems that cannot afford to disappear: hosted applications, customer networks, business services or infrastructure across data centres. Protection should be designed around those dependencies.

Public IP requirements

Bring your address plan to the discussion.

Cloudflare documents a minimum /24 publicly routable block for the standard IPv4 bring-your-own-IP approach. Cloudflare-owned address options may suit smaller requirements, subject to availability and the proposed service.

Return routing also matters: leased Cloudflare IPs use an egress design that differs from a direct-return arrangement. We confirm the complete path rather than treating every deployment as identical.

Tunnels are not all the same

GRE encapsulates traffic but does not encrypt it. IPsec provides encryption. The appropriate delivery method depends on your requirements and supported configuration.

Review failover, tunnel health and equipment capacity as part of acceptance testing.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Can I add protection to Axclusive DIA or IP transit?+
Discuss it with us. We review the circuit, addressing, traffic and delivery requirements before confirming the managed protection design.
Do I need my own /24?+
The standard IPv4 BYOIP model requires an eligible publicly routable /24 or larger block. Cloudflare-owned address options may be available; suitability and the associated routing design must be confirmed.
Does Magic Transit replace a WAF or endpoint security?+
No. Network DDoS protection is one part of a wider security design. Application security, identity controls and endpoint protection need their own scope.
Will it guarantee no latency change or downtime?+
No universal promise is appropriate. Assess the delivery path and test the proposed configuration against agreed performance and availability requirements.
Is the price fixed regardless of configuration?+
Pricing and inclusions follow the proposal. Confirm protected prefixes, traffic measurement, delivery, service modes, add-ons and managed support.
