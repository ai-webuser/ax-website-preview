A phone system without the day-to-day burden

Built around how a smaller business answers calls.

A managed cloud PBX is a hosted business phone system operated for you under an agreed support scope. Yeastar provides the PBX platform; Axclusive supplies the SIP trunk and manages your cloud-based system.

SMEs are the primary audience, especially teams without a dedicated telephony administrator. Multi-site and growing businesses can also discuss suitability based on users, concurrent calls, integrations and support needs.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Is this managed PBX service only for SMEs?+
The page focuses on SMEs because many want business calling without running a PBX themselves. Growing and multi-site businesses can also discuss a design based on their users, call demand and operational needs.
Who supplies the SIP trunk and manages the PBX?+
Axclusive provides the SIP trunk as an IMDA-licensed IP telephony provider and manages the Yeastar cloud PBX under the agreed service scope.
What is the standard support arrangement?+
Standard managed PBX support is 8×5×4: eight support hours a day, five days a week, with a four-hour response target during the agreed coverage window. Optional 24×7×4 support extends coverage to every hour of the week. Response is not the same as resolution.
Is number porting included?+
No. Number porting is charged separately and depends on eligibility, ownership details and coordination with your current provider.
Do we need to buy new desk phones?+
Share the existing phone models and firmware for compatibility review. Supported Linkus clients may also be suitable. Device purchase, provisioning and support are confirmed in your proposal.
Can Yeastar work with Microsoft Teams?+
Yeastar supports different Teams integration approaches. Its Linkus integration within Teams differs from native Teams Phone calling. We review the desired user experience, supported version and licences before proposing an integration.
What happens if our office Internet fails?+
Cloud hosting does not remove the need for working connectivity at the user’s device. Discuss an alternative connection, mobile working or configured call fallback as part of the service design.
