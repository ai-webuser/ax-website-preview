What is Webex Calling?

Cloud calling for the business number people already know.

Webex Calling is Cisco’s cloud business calling platform. With the appropriate subscription, PSTN connection and supported endpoints, users can handle external telephone calls through the configured Webex environment.

A meeting experience and a business telephone service have different requirements. We start with who answers your calls, how callers reach the right team and how the service will be operated.

Connect the platform to your voice provider

Choose a supported PSTN design.

Webex Calling supports different PSTN connection models. A supported local gateway can connect a premises-based PSTN service with Webex Calling; the gateway, licensing and configuration must fit the proposed deployment.

Axclusive will confirm the integration used for your service. Our SIP provider role does not mean every Cisco PSTN option or gateway is included automatically.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Is “Cisco Teams” the product name?+
This service uses Cisco Webex Calling. Microsoft Teams Calling is a separate option, with different subscriptions and integration requirements.
Does a Webex meetings subscription include external phone calls?+
Do not assume it does. Webex Calling licensing and an appropriate PSTN connection are required for the proposed external calling service.
Can Axclusive supply the telephone connectivity?+
Yes. Axclusive is an IMDA-licensed IP telephony provider. We confirm the supported PSTN integration for your Webex Calling deployment.
Can we keep our current business numbers?+
Eligible number porting is subject to checks and coordination with the current provider. Porting is charged separately and scheduled with the deployment.
Can we reuse existing Cisco phones?+
Share the exact models, firmware and current registration platform. Supportability and any migration or licensing requirements must be checked before reuse.
How is the overall price calculated?+
The quote should identify Webex subscriptions, voice channels and numbers, usage, any gateway or device costs, implementation, porting and support. The SIP calculator covers only the voice component.
