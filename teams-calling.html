Meetings and telephone calls are different

Give Teams users a route to the telephone network.

Microsoft Teams Calling lets users make and receive external telephone calls through an appropriately configured Teams Phone deployment. A Teams chat or meeting subscription alone does not establish public telephone connectivity.

Start with the user experience you need: native Teams Phone calling, an existing PBX alongside Teams, or a managed PBX with an integration inside Teams. Those choices lead to different designs and licence requirements.

The complete call path matters

Plan more than the calling licence.

List users, phone numbers, shared devices and departments that handle inbound calls. Then review tenant access, the supported integration, network readiness and number migration.

For Direct Routing, Microsoft requires an appropriately licensed Teams Phone environment and a certified SBC. The proposed deployment identifies who provides and manages each component.

A staged migration

Pilot with a small group, test the calls they actually make, then plan the wider change. Include transfers, caller ID, voicemail, inbound routing and the agreed emergency calling arrangements.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Is Microsoft Teams Calling the same as a Teams meeting?+
No. A Teams meeting connects participants through the collaboration service. Calling external telephone numbers requires an appropriate calling licence, PSTN connection and configuration.
Do we need Teams Phone licences?+
Native Teams Phone Direct Routing requires Microsoft Teams and Teams Phone licensing. Other PBX integrations can have different requirements. We confirm the exact approach and subscriptions before quoting.
Can we retain our business numbers?+
Eligible numbers may be ported following ownership checks and coordination with the current provider. Porting is charged separately and scheduled as part of the migration.
Can Axclusive provide the voice connection?+
Yes. Axclusive is licensed by IMDA to provide IP telephony services. The proposal identifies the voice connection, supported Teams integration and operating responsibilities.
Can we keep some users on an existing PBX?+
A coexistence design may be suitable. Share the PBX details, user groups and routing requirements so compatibility and the migration path can be assessed.
Does the SIP calculator show the complete Teams price?+
No. It estimates the SIP component using its current configuration options. Microsoft licences, integration, equipment, implementation and support must be considered separately.
