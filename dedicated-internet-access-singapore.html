The answer, in plain language

What is dedicated internet access?

Dedicated Internet Access (DIA) is an Internet connection with bandwidth committed to your business, rather than shared access capacity. A dedicated internet access service combines agreed capacity with defined performance and support commitments.

For your team, the decision is practical: how much capacity do you need, what happens when something goes wrong, and who helps you resolve it?

Business dedicated internet. Two commitment options.

A dedicated internet line for business, with a commitment that fits.

Choose fixed committed bandwidth for steady requirements, or a burstable commitment when demand varies. Both options are Dedicated Internet Access; the difference is how you plan capacity and pay for usage.

Fixed bandwidth commitment

Plan around a known capacity and recurring charge.

Agree a bandwidth level for your business and a recurring service charge for that commitment. A fixed option suits predictable demand and teams that prioritise budget certainty.

Capacity: Plan within the agreed bandwidth. Discuss a service upgrade when your requirements grow.

Billing: The agreed fixed bandwidth charge, with installation, equipment and any additional services quoted separately.

Best fit: Steady workloads and a clear capacity budget.

Discuss fixed bandwidth ↗
Burstable bandwidth commitment

Commit to a baseline. Make room for peaks.

Agree a minimum bandwidth commitment and a higher burst ceiling. Use additional capacity within that ceiling when demand rises, with billing based on the agreed 95th-percentile method.

Capacity: Burst above your committed level up to the agreed service or port limit.

Billing: Your minimum commitment applies. When measured 95th-percentile usage exceeds it, additional charges follow the agreed rates.

Best fit: Variable demand, occasional traffic peaks and growth that needs headroom.

  • Your office or data centre address

  • Renewal date or preferred activation date
  • IP addressing, equipment and backup requirements

Need room for occasional peaks?

With 95th-percentile billing, bandwidth is sampled throughout the billing period. Samples are ranked and the highest 5% are excluded; the highest remaining sample sets the 95th-percentile usage level. Your minimum commitment still applies, with additional usage charged under the agreed terms. Brief peaks may fall within the excluded samples, but sustained higher usage can raise your bill. Confirm the sampling interval, treatment of inbound and outbound traffic, burst ceiling and rates in your quotation.

Dedicated Internet Access FAQ

Details for your next connectivity decision.

Should I choose fixed or burstable dedicated internet access?+
Choose fixed committed bandwidth when demand is steady and you want a known recurring bandwidth charge. Choose a burstable commitment when you need headroom above a baseline and can accommodate usage-based charges. Both are DIA options; compare your traffic pattern, minimum commitment, burst ceiling and billing terms.
What speeds does Axclusive offer for DIA?+
Axclusive offers symmetrical DIA bandwidth from 1Gbps to 100Gbps. The final service configuration and availability depend on your location and requirements.
How is dedicated internet access pricing calculated for an office in Singapore?+
The quote depends on your address, required capacity, delivery work and contract terms. Ask for recurring charges, installation costs, equipment and any burst charges to be shown separately.
What does the 99.99% uptime SLA cover?+
The service agreement sets the covered service, availability calculation, exclusions and applicable remedies. Ask the team to walk through these details for your proposed connection; an SLA is not a guarantee of uninterrupted access to every Internet destination.
Can Axclusive support multiple offices and managed services?+
Discuss DIA requirements for each office together with your wider network needs. Managed services and private connectivity can be considered separately as part of the design. Read about managed services ↗
Do I need an ASN or my own IP addresses?+
Tell the team whether you already manage an ASN, BGP and your own address space. The appropriate DIA or IP transit configuration depends on your requirements; confirm addressing and routing responsibilities in the proposal.
How long does installation take?+
Timing depends on location, circuit availability, building access and any third-party work. Request a confirmed delivery plan for your address and coordinate it with your existing contract or renewal date.
Is DDoS protection included with DIA?+
Do not assume security services are included in the connectivity price. Discuss protection requirements and confirm what is included or separately quoted. Explore DDoS protection ↗
