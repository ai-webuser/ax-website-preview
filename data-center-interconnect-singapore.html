What is DCI?

A dedicated path between your data centre environments.

Data Center Interconnect (DCI) links infrastructure in separate facilities so systems can exchange data over an agreed private connection. The right design depends on what must move, how quickly and what happens if a path fails.

Begin with the application requirement. A nightly backup, a migration and synchronous replication can have very different capacity and latency needs.

Design around the job

Three workloads. Different requirements.

Connect the recovery environment with the required capacity and diversity. Application replication and recovery procedures still determine the achievable recovery outcome.

One link is not physical diversity

Two logical services may still share a cable, building entry or device. Where continuity matters, request an explicit review of shared risks and diverse paths.

DCI performance does not by itself guarantee an application’s recovery point or recovery time.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Is DCI the same as Internet access?+
No. DCI connects agreed private endpoints. DIA provides access to public Internet destinations. A design can include both when the workload needs them.
What speeds can I discuss?+
Share your current and projected demand. Axclusive’s existing DCI offering includes 1–100Gbps options; availability and any higher-capacity design are confirmed for the endpoints.
Does every circuit include encryption?+
No. Encryption is a separate design requirement. Confirm the supported method, equipment and scope in the proposal.
Can you guarantee a specific latency?+
A latency target must be assessed for the actual endpoints and proposed path, then stated in the applicable service agreement. Do not assume one figure applies to every route.
What affects DCI pricing?+
Endpoints, capacity, facility cross-connects, handoff equipment, contract term, encryption and diversity all affect the quote.
