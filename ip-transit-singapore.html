Your questions, answered

IP Transit FAQ

A clearer path to the right connection.

What is IP transit?+
IP transit is a service that gives your network access to the global Internet through a provider’s network. It carries traffic beyond the networks you connect to directly, helping your users and services reach Internet destinations.
How is IP transit different from dedicated internet access?+
The right choice depends on your network design and who manages routing and addressing. IP transit often serves networks with their own ASN and IP prefixes, while dedicated internet access can suit businesses looking for a managed Internet connection. Product definitions vary, so confirm the routing, address and delivery arrangements for your service. Explore dedicated internet access in Singapore.
How does IP transit differ from peering?+
Peering lets two networks exchange traffic for the destinations covered by their agreement. IP transit provides onward access to the wider Internet. Many networks use both: peering for selected traffic and transit for broader reach. Learn how peering works.
What is blended IP transit?+
Blended IP transit combines connectivity from multiple upstream providers within one service. Axclusive uses multi-homed Tier 1 and Tier 2 upstream connectivity alongside peering. Ask how the proposed service supports your key destinations and resilience requirements.
Do I need my own ASN and IP addresses?+
Requirements depend on the service and routing arrangement. If you already have an ASN and IP prefixes, include them in your enquiry. If you do not, describe your network and applications so the team can advise on a suitable connectivity option.
Can Axclusive provide China-optimized BGP routing?+
Yes. China-optimized BGP routing is available. Share the destinations, applications and traffic requirements that matter to your network so our team can discuss the routing option and service configuration for your connection.
How much does IP transit in Singapore cost?+
A quote depends on delivery location, capacity, service configuration and commercial terms. Share your required bandwidth and location, then confirm the billing model, setup fees and cross-connect costs alongside the recurring charge.
What should I prepare before requesting a quote?+
Start with your service address or data centre, desired bandwidth and activation date. ASN and IP-prefix information, traffic destinations and resilience requirements are helpful if you already know them. You can still enquire while these details are being worked out.
