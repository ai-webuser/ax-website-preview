What should a managed service do?

Turn a list of devices into a clear operating responsibility.

A managed service assigns agreed operational tasks to a provider. Its value depends on what is covered, who owns decisions and how work is measured—not simply on having a support telephone number.

Axclusive combines enterprise IT experience with operation of its own network. Our certified Cisco Networking Partner and certified Managed Services Practice credentials support a conversation spanning equipment, connectivity and ongoing operations.

Service levels that mean something

Separate coverage, response and resolution.

Support hours tell you when the service operates. A response target sets when a request is acknowledged or acted upon under the agreement. Resolution depends on the incident and the agreed obligations.

Confirm severity definitions, escalation, maintenance windows, reporting and exclusions. Managed PBX support packages do not automatically define the terms for every managed IT service.

A useful service proposal includes

The asset and site list; recurring activities; change approval process; support coverage; response targets; vendor dependencies; backup and recovery scope; reporting; and exit or handover requirements.

Questions before you switch

Business calling FAQ

Clear requirements make for a better migration.

Can you manage only part of our environment?+
Yes. Discuss a defined scope alongside your internal team or other providers. The proposal identifies assets, responsibilities and exclusions.
Are monitoring and backups always included?+
No universal inclusion is assumed. Monitoring, backup jobs, restoration, updates and reporting must be stated for the systems covered.
Does your Cisco certification apply to every vendor?+
Our stated Cisco credentials relate to that programme. Other platforms and technologies are assessed and scoped separately; no additional vendor certification is implied.
What support hours are available?+
Coverage and response commitments are agreed for the particular service. The published managed PBX packages are specific to that offering, not a blanket commitment for all IT support.
Can connectivity be included?+
Discuss Axclusive connectivity alongside equipment and operations. The proposal separates circuit commitments from the managed service responsibilities.
